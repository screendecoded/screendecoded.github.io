### Importance of infinite contrast

Infinite contrast means pixels can switch fully off, so black is truly black, right next to bright highlights. Its importance cannot be overstated: it is the single biggest factor in a TV's picture quality. OLED is the only technology that offers this at home at an affordable price today.

One common question is whether to buy a top-end LCD TV instead of an OLED for everyday viewing. The answer is: it depends. How much do you value real black when the screen should be black, and the fine contrast between brightness and colors?

If you have experienced infinite contrast before, it's really hard to go back to a TV without it, even if that TV is otherwise great, like a top-of-the-line LCD TV. No matter how great the latest LCD TVs with RGB backlights are, they are still LCD TVs and lack infinite contrast.

By now, most people have experienced infinite contrast in some form. Their phone's screen is most likely self-emissive, like OLED, where every pixel makes its own light. If you want that amazing picture on your big screen, it has to be a self-emissive screen with infinite contrast. There's no way around it.

For now, infinite contrast comes with a risk of burn-in. That's a decision you as the TV buyer have to make, and it's the biggest decision of them all, because it alone decides what kind of viewing experience you get.

This is also one of those things where you don't know what you are missing until you have experienced it. Once you have, it's really hard to go back.
